Inside the Gear Locker

Professional tools, selected with purpose

My equipment is chosen around the environment, subject, and final image. Scroll through the core kit that carries projects from wide night skies to distant wildlife.

01 / Camera Body

Nikon D800

36.3 MP · Full-frame FX · Nikon F-mount

The D800 is the foundation of my photography kit. Its high-resolution full-frame sensor provides the detail and flexibility I need for landscapes, portraits, action, and commercial work.

02 / Complete System

Built around the project

Camera · Lens · Grip · Field support

The camera is only one part of the system. Lenses and supporting equipment are selected around the subject, location, working distance, and final use of the photographs.

03 / Ultra-wide

Nikon 14–24mm f/2.8G

Astrophotography · Landscapes · Environments

An ultra-wide professional zoom used primarily for night skies, expansive landscapes, and scenes where the surrounding environment is an essential part of the story.

04 / Everyday range

Nikon 28–70mm f/2.8D

Everyday work · Portraits · Events

My everyday professional zoom covers natural portraits, documentary moments, events, and the changing situations that make up a typical assignment.

05 / Telephoto zoom

Nikon 70–200mm f/2.8G VR

Action · Portraits · Compressed landscapes

A fast telephoto zoom that lets me isolate subjects and stay close to the action without interrupting it. It is a mainstay for outdoor sports, portraits, and events.

06 / Super-telephoto

Nikon AF-I 300mm f/2.8D

Wildlife · Distant subjects · Outdoor action

Passed down from my grandfather, this professional super-telephoto is both a working creative tool and a meaningful part of my collection. Its reach and bright aperture make it ideal for wildlife and long-distance photography.
