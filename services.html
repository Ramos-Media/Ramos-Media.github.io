Project-Based Pricing

A useful estimate starts with the details.

Every project has different creative and production needs. Instead of forcing your idea into a fixed package, I review the scope and provide an estimate based on the work involved.

  • Project scope and creative requirements
  • Time, location, and travel
  • Number of images or amount of footage
  • Editing complexity and final deliverables
  • Timeline and revision needs
